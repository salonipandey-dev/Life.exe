# 15 User manual

## Getting started
1. Open LIFE.EXE and choose **Create account**. Your browser's time zone is detected automatically (change it later in Settings).
2. You land on the **Command Center**. The ring shows your level; the gold arc is your progress toward the next level.

## Quests
- **New quest** (Command Center or Quest Board). Give it a concrete title, pick a difficulty and optionally a due date, repeat rule, skill it trains and a "done when" line. The XP it's worth is shown and comes from difficulty: Easy 15, Medium 35, Hard 70, Epic 150.
- **Start** when you begin, **Complete** when the "done when" is true. You get XP and coins once; pressing Complete again does nothing.
- Made a mistake? Open the quest and **Reopen**: the XP goes back to the ledger and you can complete it again later.
- Repeating quests (every day / every week) can be completed once per day or week.
- Overdue quests are marked but never punished: completing late still earns full XP.
- **Pause**, **Cancel quest**, **Mark expired** and **Restore** are in the quest's detail view. Finished quests can be **archived**; quests with history can't be deleted, because your XP is based on them.
- There's a daily XP limit (1,500). Anything beyond it is still recorded as done, just with reduced XP.

## Habits
- **New habit**: every day, on specific days, or a number of times per week; tracked as done/not done, a count, or minutes.
- Tap the circle (or enter a value and **Log**) to record today. Each completed day earns 5 XP.
- **Take a rest day** or **Pause** when you need to; neither breaks your streak. A missed day ends the current streak but never costs XP.
- You can correct the last 30 days.

## Skills
- Each skill has two meters: **Practice XP** (from quests linked to the skill) and **Evidence** (things you made). A skill is **Demonstrated** only when both are met, and that unlocks the skills that build on it.
- Open a skill to add evidence (a title and optional link) or prerequisites. Circular prerequisites are refused.
- **Add a skill** to create your own, in an existing or new tree.

## Analytics
Pick a range (7, 28 or 90 days). Everything is calculated from what you recorded, by your local day. Expand an insight to see the numbers behind it; open "How these metrics are defined" for exact definitions. Use **Show table** for the data behind the XP chart.

## Settings
Change display name, avatar, title (unlocks with level), theme (Obsidian or Ivory), time zone and the hour your day starts (work before it counts toward the previous day). You can also change your password (signs out other devices), **Download my data**, sign out, or delete your account permanently.

## Keyboard and accessibility
All actions are reachable by keyboard; Tab shows a focus ring and a "Skip to content" link. Dialogs close with Escape. Motion is reduced automatically if your system asks for it.
